# Privacy Policy - Emindi Bro Shorts

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Emindi Bro Shorts is a personal automation project (source code: https://github.com/chaithanyanandam/Auto_You) that generates short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel (@EmindiBro).

## What the app does
- Uses the YouTube Data API v3 with OAuth 2.0 to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
- The OAuth access is used only by the channel owner's own automation scripts.

## What data we collect
- None. The app does not collect, store, or share any user data.
- It never accesses any Google account other than the channel owner's, and never reads data from viewers or subscribers.

## Third-party services
- YouTube Data API v3 (video uploads)
- Pexels / Pixabay APIs (royalty-free stock media)
- Openverse (CC0 music)
